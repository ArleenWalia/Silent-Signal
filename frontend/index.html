What is this

Not a journal.
Not a therapy app.

Universities track grades, retention rates, and graduation numbers. Nobody tracks the quiet cost of getting there. The skipped meals, the clubs you couldn't join, the help you didn't ask for because it felt pointless.

Silent Signal is an anonymous check-in that takes 90 seconds. You're not reflecting on your mindset. You're reporting a friction point — something the system put in your way. We aggregate those signals across students and make the pattern visible.

The insight isn't "what's wrong with you." It's "you're not the exception — this is the rule." That distinction matters. It shifts shame into evidence. And evidence can move things.

"68% of second-year students couldn't attend events they wanted to this semester. That's not a personal failure. That's data."

🔒

Fully anonymous

No account. No email. No tracking. You submit a signal and it dissolves into the aggregate — indistinguishable from anyone else's.

📡

Systemic, not personal

We ask about barriers the institution created — not your mental state. No diagnosis, no labels, no clinical framing. Just: what got in your way?

📊

Collective visibility

Your signal joins hundreds of others. The dashboard shows real patterns — by year, program, and week. Invisible suffering becomes undeniable data.

🎯

Routes to action

Aggregated data goes to student unions and campus newspapers — bodies with actual incentive to act, not the institution being criticized.
