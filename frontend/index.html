What is Silent Signal

The voice of students.
Backed by data.

Every year, universities promise their students community, growth, and support. Every year, the structure they've built makes those promises nearly impossible to keep. Silent Signal exists to make that gap visible — and impossible to ignore.

We built an anonymous 90-second check-in tool that captures student friction — not feelings, not diagnoses, but systemic barriers: workload, isolation, inaccessible resources, the quiet cost of just keeping up. Students submit signals. We aggregate them. The pattern becomes undeniable data.

Our AI doesn't just analyze — it reframes. When a student submits "I couldn't keep up," our system says: "79% of your peers reported the same thing this week. This is a structural problem, not a personal one." That distinction matters. It shifts shame into evidence.

Aggregated AI-generated reports are routed to student unions, campus newspapers, and faculty councils — the bodies with actual incentive to act. We believe in actionable data going to the people who will use it.

"Students aren't failing to thrive. They're succeeding at surviving a system that was never designed for them."
