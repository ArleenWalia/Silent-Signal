What this is

Not a journal.
Not a therapy app.

Universities track grades and graduation numbers. Nobody tracks the quiet cost of getting there.

Silent Signal is an anonymous check-in that takes 90 seconds. You're not reflecting on your mindset — you're reporting a friction point. Something the system put in your way.

Our AI reads the aggregate patterns and generates real insights — not generic wellness tips, but data-backed evidence that the system is failing, not you.

"68% of second-year students couldn't attend events they wanted to this semester. That's not a personal failure. That's data."
